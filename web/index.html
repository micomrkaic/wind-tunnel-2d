wind-tunnel-2d

Two 2D CFD solvers for the same tunnel: particle MD and lattice Boltzmann. Live demos in your browser; the headless validator runs natively.

interactive demos

lbm_tunnel — BGK toy

Single-relaxation-time D2Q9 BGK. Stable to about Mach 0.30; drag it higher and watch it fall apart. That failure is the point.

open →

lbm_pro — validated TRT

Same shell, validated solver: TRT collision, Bouzidi curved walls, Guo 2nd-order BCs, momentum-exchange forces. Live Cd/Cl on the HUD. Cylinder only — the only geometry Bouzidi is validated on.

open →

native-only

wind_tunnel (particle MD, Mach 2+ through a choked nozzle) and lbm_bench (headless Schaefer-Turek validator, reproduces Cd=3.22, St=0.30 to within 1% at res=40) are not built for the browser on purpose — the first is CPU-heavy, the second is a validator and produces numbers, not pictures. Build them natively from the repository.
